A small mobile game we didn't have enough time to finish during our year's
work experience. A fun little game in which the goal is to avoid being hit by
heat seeking rockets. Gain boost by doing risky / cool things such as getting
close misses, rockets closely tailing you or even destroying rockets by
allowing them to crash into each other.

Click and drag with your mouse to move your player around, hold right click to
use your boost.
