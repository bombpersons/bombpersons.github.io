As part of an AI module in the final year, I created this simple 2D shooter as
a test for implementing AI planning (based on GOAP, originally used in
F.E.A.R). The goal was to have a top down arena style shooter with AI that
would duck for cover, acquire ammunition and health, and seek out and eliminate
competition.
